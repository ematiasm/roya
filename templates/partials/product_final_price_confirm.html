{# The CONFIRM half of the final-price control (final price markup U2). This partial is rendered ONLY by `/web/product-final-price` and its confirm route, and it is swapped into `#product-final-price-confirm` out of band. It is deliberately NOT part of the drawer's own markup: a drawer that has never been previewed, and a preview that was refused, both leave the slot empty and neither offers a way to write. "Preview, then confirm" is therefore a property of the markup rather than of a disabled attribute somebody can work around with the keyboard. The button is a `type="button"` carrying its own `hx-post`, which is what makes the two steps two steps: it never submits the control's form, and the control's form is the PREVIEW. So a stray Enter, or the Preview button, can only ever preview. `hx-include` is a comma-separated selector list — htmx resolves `hx-include` with `querySelectorAll` — and it names BOTH forms: the control's own, for the final price, and the save form, for the product id and the cost the operator has on screen. The confirm is handed VALUES, never a previewed number. #} {% if enabled %}

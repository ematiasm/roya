{# The product price ladder (product price ladder U2): ONE ordered set of price facts, in the order an operator can sanity-check a price — cost, markup, net sale price, each tax with its rate and amount, total taxes, tax-inclusive price. This is the only place the products screen shows money. Everything here is computed on the server, through the two contracts the save and a document line already use (`inventory::derive_net_sale_price` and `line_taxes::calculate_line_taxes`), and formatted here in the active locale. The browser computes nothing: a `change` on the cost, markup or sale price field asks `/web/product-price-ladder` for this fragment back, and htmx swaps `outerHTML` so the island replaces itself. Two states are part of the answer rather than an error, because the ladder's job is to say what a save WOULD do before the operator saves: * `net_refusal` — the save path refuses this exact input, so the ladder states its own refusal — through the same mapping the product save form uses, so the two can never word it differently or in different languages — and publishes no tax money at all. There is no net price for a tax to apply to. * `inputs_unreadable` — a field is not a number yet, so the ladder reports the last state the save path accepted and says that is what it is showing. Every utility class here already exists in the committed stylesheet: the vocabulary is the removed breakdown table's and the cards' own. #}

Purchase Detail

Click View on a purchase to see lines and payments.

New Purchase (Draft)

Draft touches no stock, no finance and no satellite cost. Also at POST /api/purchases

Add Line (Draft only)

Unknown product ⇒ 404, qty ≤ 0 ⇒ 400, same product twice ⇒ 400. Also at POST /api/purchases/:id/lines

Confirm Purchase

Assigns YYYY-PURCH-NNNNNN, receives stock, updates the satellite cost; Cash posts 1 Expense. Disallowed pair ⇒ 400, no stock/finance touch. Double confirm ⇒ 400.

Record Payment (Credit)

Each payment posts 1 Expense with method (N per purchase, mixed accounts/methods, sum ≤ total). Disallowed pair ⇒ 400, no finance touch. Overpay ⇒ 400.

Cancel Purchase

Draft ⇒ discard, no side effects. Confirmed ⇒ stock back (Purchase-return) + Income refund per payment.
